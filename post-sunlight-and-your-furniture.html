Sunlight and Your Furniture

May 12, 2026 · 5 min read · by the Heritage & Grain bench

UV fades timber unevenly. A few simple habits keep colour consistent for decades.

Rotate table leaves and move objects on a surface every few months so colour shifts evenly.

UV film on windows cuts fading dramatically without changing how the room looks.

Walnut lightens in sun while cherry darkens — plan placement around the timber you own.
